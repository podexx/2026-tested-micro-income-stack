## A sensible way to use the stack

1. Start with the no-extra-spending options: step rewards and carefully reviewed data/tasks.
2. Use Upside only on purchases you already planned.
3. Treat TikTok as a creator business experiment with uncertain returns, not a task app or guaranteed paycheck.
4. For mileage, separate recordkeeping value from any promotional reward.
5. After 30 days, total actual withdrawable cash, not app points or estimated balances, and divide by the time spent. Drop anything that isn’t worth its permissions and effort.

## Legitimacy and safety checklist

- Download apps from official app stores and verify the publisher/domain.
- Never pay an upfront fee to access a promised job or release earnings.
- Read minimum cash-out, expiration, and account eligibility terms before you start.
- Protect location, health, browsing, and other personal data; deny permissions that aren’t necessary for the feature you want.
- Keep screenshots or records of completed offers and payments, and never assume a referral link guarantees a reward.
